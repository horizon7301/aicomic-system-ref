# 《开局挂科？我的绩点系统满级了》 AI漫剧: Project Review (复盘)
Status: **Paused** on 2026-10-20 (may resume later as a personal project, not for money)

## What was done
- Series bible, cast (陈鱼, 胡承泽, 徐婉晴, 霍铭, 周烈, 老秦), and a 17-panel storyboard for Ep1
- Character sheets and an arena background for the FIBA-correct court
- Ep1 panels P1–P11 drawn (P10 reuses the P9 base); P12–P17 not started
- Tried a local ComfyUI + Animagine setup on an RTX 5070 laptop (installed after fixing CUDA 12.8 / torch issues). The results looked worse than cloud generation, so it was dropped.

## Why it was paused
- Even a "passable" episode took about 20–30 images plus repeated fixes (court geometry, cloned faces, duplicate jersey numbers, floating characters, hidden eyes, brand logos)
- Time, energy and usage costs were high, and earnings only come after many episodes
- Continuing would have meant paying for extra AI subscriptions or usage before the project made any money

## Lessons learned
- Lock character sheets first and reuse base images (one image can cover two panels)
- Put UI, chat and text overlays in editing, not in generation
- Wide crowd and court shots are the hardest; keep them few
- Know the cost per episode before committing to a series
